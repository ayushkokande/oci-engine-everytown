title: Building a simple funding pipeline and diversifying revenue

Start by writing down every confirmed and potential source of funding for the next 6-12 months in
one table: source, type (government, foundation, individual, earned, in-kind), amount, likelihood,
decision date and owner. Review it every month; this single list answers "where will our funding
come from?" and shows gaps early.

Reduce dependence on one source over time. A practical first step is to name the share of the
budget that comes from the largest funder and set a modest target to lower it, for example by
adding one new foundation relationship and a small individual-giving effort this year.

Individual giving can start small: a list of current supporters, a thank-you within 48 hours of
every gift, two or three updates a year that share stories and results with participants' consent,
and one simple ask campaign. Track supporters in a spreadsheet or free CRM before buying software.

For grants, keep a reusable "boilerplate" folder (mission, programs, logic model, budget, staff
bios, outcomes) so each application takes hours rather than days.
