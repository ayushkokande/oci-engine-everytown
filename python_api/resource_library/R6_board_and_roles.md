title: Clarifying roles, decision-making and board support

source: Starter entry written for the MVP (general nonprofit practice). Replace with cleared CVI material.

Many capacity gaps come down to unclear ownership. For each core function (planning, fundraising,
finance, data and reporting), write down who leads it, who supports it, and who decides. A simple
responsibility chart prevents work from falling between staff and board.

Boards of small organizations can help most by: reviewing finances monthly or quarterly, opening
doors to funders and partners, approving the budget and key policies, and supporting the executive
director. Give board members specific, time-bound asks (for example "introduce us to two potential
funders this quarter") rather than general requests to help with fundraising.

Protect frontline staff time. Community violence intervention work is demanding, so build planning,
finance and data routines that are short, predictable and shared, rather than relying on one
person's evenings and weekends.
