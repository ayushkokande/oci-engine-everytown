Internal controls protect the organization, its staff and its funders. Even a team of three can put
the basics in place:

- Separate duties where possible: the person who approves a payment should not be the only person
  who records it and reconciles the bank account. If staff is too small, ask a board member to review
  bank statements monthly.
- Require two approvals (or a board-member approval) above a set dollar amount.
- Reconcile every bank account monthly and keep the reconciliation on file.
- Keep receipts and documentation for every expense charged to a grant.
- Write a one-page financial policies document covering approvals, reimbursements, credit cards,
  petty cash and participant stipends, and review it yearly.

These steps also prepare the organization for an audit or a funder's financial review, which
larger grants often require.
